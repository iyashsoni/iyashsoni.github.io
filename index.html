Born curious.
Built different.

I'm Yash — raised in Ahmedabad, forged in Bangalore. A BE in IT from VGEC and an MTech from IIIT-Bangalore later, I've spent a decade turning hard problems into elegant systems.

Today I'm a Software Engineer Lead at IBM, spearheading Cloud Object Storage. When I'm not deep in architecture decisions, you'll find me obsessing over markets, meditating on existence, hunting for the perfect 90s track, or asking questions that have no clean answers.
