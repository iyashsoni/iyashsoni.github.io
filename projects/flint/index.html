Features

Simple tools for
lasting change.

✔
Daily check-ins
Mark habits done or skipped — one tap, no confirmation dialogs. Skips happen; they don’t break your streak.
🔥
Honest streaks
Missing a day breaks your streak. That’s the point. A streak is only valuable if it’s real.
📅
Activity heatmap
A 4-month GitHub-style calendar showing your history at a glance. Pattern recognition at its simplest.
🔔
Daily reminders
Optional per-habit notifications at a time you choose. Never nagging — only when you want them.
📴
Offline-first PWA
All data lives in IndexedDB on your device. Works with no network. Add to home screen for a native feel.
🔐
Zero accounts
No sign-up, no server, no data ever leaving your device. Uninstalling the app deletes your data. Period.
Design principles

Radical simplicity.

1
One screen, one job
The home screen shows today’s habits. Nothing else competes for attention.
2
Friction-free logging
Marking a habit done is a single tap. No confirmation dialogs, no loading spinners.
3
Honest streaks
Skipping a day breaks your streak. That’s the point — the streak is only valuable if it’s real.
4
Your data, your device
Everything is stored in the browser’s IndexedDB. There is no server to breach.
5
Works everywhere
Built to run on any modern mobile browser without crashing. No exotic CSS, no unguarded APIs.
