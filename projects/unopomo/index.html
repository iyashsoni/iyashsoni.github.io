features

everything a focus timer
should be.

⏱
3 timer modes
focus (25 min), breather (5 min), recharge (15 min). cycle through them or let them auto-advance.
🎵
ambient sounds
each theme has a matching ambient soundscape. looping audio with volume control — set the mood and go deep.
📊
session stats
today’s count, current streak, all-time total, 7-day bar chart. see your focus patterns over time.
🌐
8 languages
english, spanish, french, german, portuguese, chinese, japanese, korean. auto-detects your browser language.
📴
offline pwa
installable on any device. works with no internet. all data stays in localstorage — no accounts, no backend.
🔒
100% private
everything lives in your browser. no tracking, no analytics, no data collection of any kind.
