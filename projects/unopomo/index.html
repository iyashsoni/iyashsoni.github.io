Features

Everything a focus timer
should be.

3 timer modes

Focus (25 min), breather (5 min), recharge (15 min). Cycle through them — or let them auto-advance.

Ambient soundscapes

Each theme has a matching ambient soundscape with volume control. Set the mood and go deep.

Session stats

Today's count, current streak, all-time total, 7-day bar chart. See your focus patterns over time.

8 languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean. Auto-detects your browser.

Offline PWA

Installable on any device. Works without internet. Data stays in localStorage — no accounts, no backend.

100% private

Everything lives in your browser. No tracking, no analytics, no data collection of any kind.

10 worlds to focus in.

Forest, ocean, fireplace, library, café, rain, white noise, lo-fi, deep space, and minimal. Each theme pairs a hand-tuned palette with an ambient soundscape — pick the world that fits your work.
